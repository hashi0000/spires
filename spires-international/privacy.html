Privacy

Privacy for this preview.

Spires Taxis Ltd operates this preview. There is no separate published privacy page on the main Spires Taxis website to copy, so this is a draft.

What this preview does

  • The quote and contact forms stay in this browser. They are not saved to the office database.
  • No card number is collected. Stripe is not opened.
  • Measurement is off unless you allow it, and even then nothing is sent to Google. The live Spires Taxis measurement id is refused.
  • A name, email, phone number or message is not put into a measurement event.

Needed before launch

  • A privacy notice approved for card payments, flight details and booking records.
  • How long booking records are kept.
  • The lawful reason for holding passenger and flight details.
  • Whether a separate Google measurement stream will be used. No id has been created here.
