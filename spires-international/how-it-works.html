How it works

Quote, then payment, then confirmation.

1

Journey

Pick up, destination, date, time, passengers, luggage, vehicle and flight details. One way or return. Extra stops can be added.

2

Fare

If there is no approved fare, you receive a quote. A placeholder price is never charged.

3

Payment

The full fare is paid before an International booking is confirmed. The payment state is awaiting payment, then paid.

4

Confirmation

Paid and confirmed are different. A driver is allocated later. Payment alone does not allocate a driver.

What this preview does

The quote stays in your browser. The office is not emailed. Card payment is switched off. A separate test panel on the booking page shows success, failure, a repeated notice, an unfinished checkout and a refund, using a 100 pence fixture that is not a customer price.
