# Image sources

No airport, Oxford or fleet photograph is included in this preview.

The Spires Taxis site has logos, event venues, flags and Grand Prix pictures. Those are not photographs of Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, Birmingham, Oxford landmarks, a Standard Car or a Mercedes Vito. They are not used here.

The design reference shows a Radcliffe Camera view and two silver vehicles. Those files are not licensed assets in this repository, so they are not copied into the site.

## Needed before launch

Each file needs a real photograph, the source, the licence, and permission for commercial use on this website.

- Oxford hero: Radcliffe Camera or another recognisable Oxford view.
- Heathrow Terminal 2, Terminal 3, Terminal 4 and Terminal 5, labelled so a first time visitor can tell them apart.
- Gatwick North Terminal and South Terminal.
- London Stansted main terminal.
- London Luton main terminal.
- Birmingham Airport main terminal.
- Standard Car: a genuine Spires fleet car, currently described on the Spires Taxis site as a Toyota RAV4, Toyota Corolla or similar.
- 8 seater: a genuine Mercedes Vito, or the similar people carrier that is actually used.

Until those files exist, the pages use labelled placeholders. They are not generated photographs of the airports or the fleet.
