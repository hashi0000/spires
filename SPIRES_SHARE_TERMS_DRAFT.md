# Spires Share — draft customer terms (NOT APPROVED)

**Status:** Draft for business and legal review. Do not publish. Do not treat as live terms.

This note describes the **intended product mechanics** of Spires Share (Heathrow MVP). It does not give legal advice and does not create customer rights until it is approved and published.

## What Spires Share is

Spires Share is a **pre-booked shared private hire airport transfer** operated by Spires Taxis. Customers travelling at similar times may share a vehicle from Thornhill Park & Ride to Heathrow Airport and pay a per-passenger fare.

It is **not** a bus, coach, scheduled public transport service, or street-hail taxi.

## Provisional booking

Reserving a place does **not** mean the departure is guaranteed to operate.

A place is provisional until:

1. the required number of passengers is reached, and
2. payment for every required place can be completed.

## Minimum passenger requirement

The commercial capacity for the MVP is **five passengers**.

A departure is intended to operate when five passenger places are reserved, or when a four-passenger alternative is offered and **every** passenger on that departure accepts it.

## Payment timing (manual hosted link MVP)

Customers register interest first. No payment is taken at that point.

When a journey reaches four passenger places, admin pastes one journey specific hosted £35 payment link and then explicitly opens the payment window. Passengers pay on the hosted page. Spires Taxis marks each reservation paid after checking the provider separately.

The four passenger option is never automatic. The extra £8.75 is only due if every remaining passenger accepts, then admin verifies supplement payments.

Mock card authorisation/capture is isolated and is not used on the live reservation path.

## Failure to fill

If the required passenger number is not reached by the confirmation deadline, the departure expires and does not operate. Provisional places are released. No customer charge is taken for that expired departure.

## Four-passenger alternative

If four passengers are matched, Spires may offer an equal-split fare so the same target revenue is covered.

The original per-passenger fare does **not** increase automatically. Every affected booking must explicitly accept. If anyone declines, the four-passenger option does not proceed.

## Customer cancellation

A customer may cancel a **provisional** place through the booking management process before confirmation, subject to the approved policy.

Cancellation after confirmation, including refunds of captured payments, requires an approved cancellation/refund policy. This draft does not invent that policy.

## Operator cancellation

Spires may cancel a departure for operational reasons. If payment has not been captured, the customer is not charged. Captured-payment refunds require the approved refund policy.

## Luggage

The MVP allowance is one large suitcase and one cabin bag per passenger, and five of each per vehicle, unless configuration is changed by Spires. Excess luggage may mean a place cannot be reserved on that departure.

## Late arrival and meeting point

The meeting point is Thornhill Park & Ride unless Spires later introduces an approved home-collection product. Customers should arrive in good time. Boarding close time is an operational setting and is not a guarantee of waiting.

## Departure time

Departure times are proposed by customers and matched with others. Spires may adjust a time where flight-safety rules still hold. A changed time should be notified.

## Flight responsibility

Flight number and flight time, if provided, are used only as a **safety check**. Spires Share does **not** promise that a passenger will catch their flight. Traffic, airport operations, and other delays can occur.

## Heathrow terminals

Customers select Terminal 2, 3, 4 or 5 for their own drop-off. Whether every passenger can be dropped at a different terminal on the same journey is an operational decision. This draft does not invent airport rules.

## Payment failures

If a departure reaches the passenger threshold but one payment method cannot be authorised, the departure is not confirmed and successful authorisations are not captured as a completed group charge. The affected customer may be asked to update their payment method within a limited time. If they do not, their provisional place may be released and offered to a waitlist customer, who must complete their own booking step. They are not charged automatically.

## Privacy

Public departure lists may show date, time, meeting point, Heathrow as destination, occupancy, fare and public status. They must not show names, emails, phone numbers, postcodes, flight numbers, payment details, or other passengers' information.

## Home collection, Gatwick, and returns

These are **not** part of the Heathrow MVP. Existing private-hire products remain separate.

## Approval required

Oxford City Council / Thornhill collection permission, insurance, published T&Cs, cancellation/refund policy, and online payment provider onboarding are **outside this draft** and remain launch blockers.
