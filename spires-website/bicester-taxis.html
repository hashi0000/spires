Taxis to Bicester

Bicester is about 30 minutes north of Oxford via the A34 or M40. We do regular runs to Bicester town centre, the railway station, and Bicester Village — the outlet shopping centre that draws visitors from Oxford, London and the airports.

For Bicester Village shopping trips, book a return with a pickup time so you're not queuing for a taxi with bags at the end of the day. We drop at the village entrance and pick you up when you're done.

Bicester taxi questions

How much is Oxford to Bicester?

Guide fare is £35 one way. Bicester Village is the same area — we confirm the exact price when you book.

Can you do a Bicester Village shopping return?

Yes. Book the outbound and give us an estimated return time. Message on WhatsApp when you're ready to leave and we'll send the driver.

How long does Oxford to Bicester take?

About 25–35 minutes depending on traffic and which route we take. Allow extra time on busy shopping weekends.

Do you pick up from Bicester for Heathrow?

Yes. Airport return booking from £87 for up to 4 passengers. Your Bicester pickup may affect the fare slightly — we'll confirm when you book.
