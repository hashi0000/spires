Oxford to Silverstone Circuit taxi

Pre-booked private hire from Oxford for Silverstone race weekends and other motorsport events. We confirm the fare and the return collection before you travel.

Book online WhatsApp for a quote Call now

Built around the event, not a routine airport run

Silverstone traffic does not behave like a Tuesday Heathrow transfer. Roads close, car parks fill, and pickup points can move for a Formula 1 weekend or another major meeting. You want a car that is already booked, with a driver who knows the outbound needs to leave Oxford with enough slack, and a return that is arranged before you go in.

We collect from your Oxford address. We are not affiliated with Silverstone Circuit or Formula 1. We do not have a base at the circuit. This is a pre-booked journey from Oxfordshire, quoted for your date.

What’s included once quoted

  • Outbound from Oxford to the circuit area
  • Optional pre-arranged return collection
  • Fixed fare confirmed before travel
  • Standard Car or 8 Seater for a group

There is no published Silverstone fare on the rates page. Send the event date, passenger count and whether you want the return booked too.

Race weekends and other meetings

The same booking rules apply whether you are going for the British Grand Prix, another championship round, or a smaller test or club meeting: book early, and agree how you are getting home.

Formula 1 and big events

Demand for cars around Oxford jumps on Grand Prix weekends. If you need the 8 Seater for a group, ask as soon as your tickets are confirmed. Extra stops on the way are £10 each when agreed in advance, same as our other work. See vehicles.

Pickup and drop-off on the day

We cannot promise a particular Silverstone gate. Access and traffic management change for major events. We drop as close as the circuit will allow that day, and we agree a realistic meeting plan for any return when you book, not at the roadside after the race.

Groups, the 8 Seater, and coming home

Standard Car is up to four passengers. A group sharing a box, or friends splitting the cost, usually wants the 8 Seater so nobody is left booking a second car at the last minute.

Most customers do not keep the vehicle on site all day. Book the outbound, then a return collection at a time you can actually make. If the meeting overruns, message us. Waiting after an agreed time is charged as on our rates page where it applies; we would tell you first.

Other non-airport journeys from Oxford are on long distance taxis. Business account bookings: corporate taxis.

What to send when you book

  • Pickup address in Oxford
  • Event date and, if you know it, session times
  • Number of passengers
  • Whether you want a timed return collection
  • Any extra pickup on the way

WhatsApp is the practical way to sort a race weekend. You can also use the contact form.

Silverstone FAQ

Can you drop us at a specific gate?

We cannot promise that. Event access changes. We get you as close as the circuit allows that day and agree a return meeting plan when you book.

How much is Oxford to Silverstone?

We quote a fixed fare for your date and addresses. There is no Silverstone line on the published airport rate card.

Will the car wait during the race?

Not unless we have quoted that. Most people book outbound plus a timed return. If you want the vehicle to stay, say so when you enquire.

Can a group share the 8 Seater?

Yes, subject to availability. Book early for Formula 1 weekends. See our vehicles.
