Spires Share — Pilot Service

Spires Share terms (draft)

Version identifier: share-heathrow-mvp-2026-09-28. Source: SPIRES SHARE TERMS DRAFT.

What Spires Share is

Spires Share is a pre booked shared private hire airport transfer operated by Spires Taxis. The initial pilot route is Oxford to Heathrow Airport Terminal 3 in a Mercedes Vito. It is not a bus, coach, scheduled public transport service, or street hail taxi.

Provisional place

Registering interest does not confirm your journey and no payment is taken at that stage. A place is provisional until the required number of passengers is reached and payment for every required place has been confirmed by Spires Taxis.

Fares

The standard fare is £35 per passenger when five passengers travel. If only four passengers are ready, Spires Taxis may offer a four passenger departure of £43.75 each. That option is never automatic. All four passengers must accept, all four must have paid £35, and all four must pay the £8.75 supplement before the journey can confirm.

Payment

Payment uses a hosted payment page. Returning from that page does not mean the payment has succeeded. Spires Taxis confirms each payment separately. If the journey does not fill, affected passengers will be contacted regarding their payment. Refunds during the pilot are handled manually.

Departure

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. The vehicle leaves at the stated departure time. A short grace period of up to five minutes may be possible at the driver's discretion, but this is not guaranteed. Passengers should not rely on this.

Cancellation and no show

More than 24 hours before departure, a cancellation releases the seat. Within 24 hours it is recorded as a late cancellation. A confirmed journey stays confirmed if already locked. A no show does not automatically cancel a confirmed journey. Refund or retention decisions remain with Spires Taxis during the pilot.
