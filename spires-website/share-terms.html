Spires Share Terms and Conditions

Version identifier: spires-share-pilot-2026-10.

What Spires Share is

Spires Share is a pre booked shared private hire service operated by Spires Taxis. It allows passengers travelling in a similar direction and at compatible times to register interest in sharing a vehicle.

It may include airport journeys, city journeys, event journeys and other selected long distance journeys. Registering interest does not guarantee that a journey will operate. Spires Share is not scheduled public transport, a bus, a coach, or a street hail taxi.

Interest

Customers may register interest in a route. Other customers may see aggregate interest and join. Passenger identities and private destination information are not publicly displayed.

Registering interest does not confirm a booking, does not guarantee the journey, and does not initially take payment. Interest is a request to travel approximately on that route, date and time.

Proposed journey

Once sufficient compatible interest exists, Spires Taxis may propose an exact journey. The proposal may specify the departure point, departure time, shared drop off, fare and capacity.

You may need to accept the proposal before payment is requested. Registering interest at an approximate time does not commit you to a different exact departure.

City drop off

For city journeys, customers may provide their intended final destination for matching purposes. This does not mean the vehicle will take them directly there.

A common shared drop off may be proposed. Passengers are responsible for onward travel from the agreed shared drop off. Where destination matching applies, the shared drop off is disclosed before payment. Declining a proposed drop off before payment is not treated as a late cancellation.

Heathrow

Current Oxford to Heathrow Share journeys may use Terminal 3 as the single shared Heathrow drop off. Passengers flying from another terminal are responsible for transferring from Terminal 3.

Spires provides a Heathrow terminal transfer guide. Heathrow Airport operates those transfer services. Routes and services can change. Spires Taxis does not operate Heathrow terminal transfers and does not guarantee transfer times.

Waitlist

When all places are provisionally allocated, additional customers may join a waitlist. If a seat becomes available it may be offered for a limited period. If it is not claimed, it may be offered to the next eligible passenger. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee travel.

Pricing

The fare applicable to each journey will be shown before the passenger is asked to pay. Individual routes may have different fares. Do not assume that every Spires Share journey uses the Heathrow pilot fare.

For the current Oxford to Heathrow Terminal 3 pilot, the usual fare is £35 per passenger when five passengers travel. If only four passengers are ready, Spires Taxis may offer a four passenger departure of £43.75 each. That option is never automatic. All four passengers must accept, all four must have paid £35, and all four must pay the £8.75 supplement before that journey can confirm. This four passenger fallback applies only where it is enabled for that journey. It is not applied automatically to London or other routes.

Payment

Payment uses a hosted payment page. Returning from that page does not mean the payment has succeeded. Spires Taxis confirms each payment separately. Payment by one passenger does not necessarily mean the overall journey is confirmed. If the journey does not fill, affected passengers will be contacted regarding their payment. Refunds during the pilot are handled manually.

Journey confirmation

There is a clear distinction between interest registered, proposed journey, place accepted, payment received and journey confirmed. A confirmed journey stays confirmed if a later passenger leaves, subject to the applicable terms. A released place may be offered to the waitlist.

Departure

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. The vehicle leaves at the stated departure time. A short grace period of up to five minutes may be possible at the driver's discretion, but this is not guaranteed. Passengers should not rely on this.

Cancellation and no show

Withdrawing unpaid interest is different from cancelling a paid or confirmed journey. More than 24 hours before departure, a cancellation of an unpaid or eligible reservation releases the seat. Within 24 hours it is recorded as a late cancellation. A confirmed journey stays confirmed if already locked. A no show does not automatically cancel a confirmed journey. Refund or retention decisions remain with Spires Taxis during the pilot.
