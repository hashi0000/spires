How flight monitoring works

On airport pickups, give us your flight number when you book. We track the live arrival time — if your flight is delayed or lands early, we shift the driver accordingly rather than charging you for waiting we caused by not paying attention.

Once the plane lands, we allow up to 50 minutes of complimentary waiting from the actual landing time for you to get through passport control and collect bags. After that, extra waiting is £5 per 15 minutes. Full detail: airport collection and waiting time.

Pickup, luggage and waiting

Going to the airport

We pick you up from your Oxford address at the agreed time. Allow a bit of buffer for motorway traffic on Heathrow runs — we’ll advise when you book.

Meeting you on arrival

We’ll agree where to meet — usually outside arrivals once you have your bags. Your driver’s number is sent before the journey.

Luggage

Takes up to 4 passengers with normal suitcases. Extra bags or a larger group — mention it when booking so we send the right vehicle.

Common questions

Is the price fixed when I book?

Yes. The fare we confirm is what you pay for that journey, unless you change the route or add stops — we’d tell you first.

Is airport parking included?

Yes, within normal limits. Short-stay pickup charges at the airport are covered in our fixed airport fares. Extra parking from waiting beyond the complimentary 50 minutes may be added to the fare.

How long will you wait after my flight lands?

Airport collections include flight tracking and up to 50 minutes of complimentary waiting from the actual landing time. After that, extra waiting is £5 per 15 minutes. See airport collection and waiting time.

What if my flight is badly delayed?

We track it. The driver is rescheduled to the new landing time. The complimentary 50 minutes of waiting starts from the actual landing time, not the original schedule.

Can I pay by card?

Yes — card or cash to the driver, unless you’ve arranged account billing.

Do you do return bookings?

Yes. Book outbound and return together through the website or by getting in touch.
