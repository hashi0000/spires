The journey

Journey time and distance

From central Oxford to Heathrow is approximately 45 to 50 miles, usually via the M40. In typical conditions the drive takes around 60 to 90 minutes, depending on traffic, your pickup location and which terminal you need.

We do not guarantee a journey time. Motorway congestion, roadworks and terminal approach queues can add delay, especially at peak times. When you book, we suggest a pickup time that builds in sensible buffer for your flight.

Coverage

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Oxford to Heathrow?

Our published Standard Car fare is £92 one way and £180 return when both journeys are booked together (up to 4 passengers). The 8 Seater is £120 one way or £235 return (up to 8 passengers). The price confirmed at the time of booking is the price you pay.

Is the displayed price per passenger or per vehicle?

Per vehicle for the whole party, up to the passenger limit of the car you book. It is not charged per passenger.

How long does Oxford to Heathrow take?

About 45–50 miles from central Oxford; normally around 60–90 minutes depending on traffic, pickup point and terminal. Journey time is not guaranteed.

Which Heathrow terminals do you cover?

Terminals 2, 3, 4 and 5. Tell us your terminal or flight number when you book.

What time should I leave Oxford?

We suggest a pickup time based on your flight and terminal. Allow driving time plus buffer before check-in, more for long-haul or busy periods.

Can I book a return collection from Heathrow?

Yes. Book outbound and return together. Details for arrivals: Heathrow to Oxford.

Do you monitor incoming flights?

Flight monitoring applies when we collect you from Heathrow. Give us your flight number and we adjust for delays using the actual landing time. Collection waiting is explained on how airport pickup works. For Oxford to Heathrow we pick you up from your address.

Is the airport charge included?

Yes. Our advertised airport fares include the standard airport pickup or drop off charge. See the rates page.

Can eight passengers travel together?

Yes. Book the 8 Seater (Mercedes Vito or similar). Heathrow is £120 one way or £235 return.

How much luggage can each vehicle carry?

Standard Car: up to four passengers with normal holiday bags, typically a Toyota RAV4, Toyota Corolla or similar. 8 Seater: better for larger groups or several large cases. Tell us passenger and bag counts when you book. See our vehicles.

Can students receive a discount?

Eligible Oxford University and Oxford Brookes students can receive a student discount with valid ID. Mention it when booking. More on student taxis.

How can I pay?

Card, cash and bank transfer where arranged for your booking. Account invoicing is available for regular travellers. See terms and contact.
