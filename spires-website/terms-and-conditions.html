General Terms

Booking and Service Terms

These terms apply to all bookings made with Spires Taxis Oxford.

📋

Bookings

  • All bookings are subject to driver and vehicle availability
  • We recommend booking in advance, especially for airport transfers and peak times
  • Bookings can be made by phone, WhatsApp, email, or through our Customer Portal
  • You will receive confirmation of your booking via your preferred contact method
  • Please provide accurate pickup and destination addresses, dates, and times
💰

Pricing and Payment

  • Fares are quoted at the time of booking and are based on the information provided
  • Airport transfer rates are fixed prices as displayed on our rates page
  • Payment can be made by cash, card, or contactless payment in the vehicle
  • For return journeys, you may be required to pay the first leg plus half of the second leg upfront, with the remainder paid on return
  • All prices are in British Pounds (GBP) and include VAT where applicable
❌

Cancellations and Changes

  • Please notify us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change your booking
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup may incur a cancellation fee
  • No-show charges may apply if you fail to appear at the scheduled pickup location
  • Changes to booking details (times, addresses, etc.) should be communicated promptly
  • We reserve the right to charge additional fees for significant changes to bookings
⏱️

Waiting Time and Additional Charges

  • We allow reasonable waiting time at pickup locations (typically 5-10 minutes)
  • Additional waiting time beyond the standard period may incur charges
  • Extra stops or route deviations may result in additional charges
  • Out-of-hours bookings (typically after midnight or before 6am) may incur surcharges
  • All additional charges will be communicated to you before the journey
🚗

Service Standards

  • All our drivers are licensed by Oxford City Council
  • Vehicles are regularly maintained and insured for private hire
  • We aim to arrive on time, but cannot guarantee exact arrival times due to traffic conditions
  • Flight monitoring is included for airport transfers
  • Luggage assistance is provided as standard
👥

Passenger Responsibilities

  • Passengers must be ready at the scheduled pickup time
  • Please provide accurate contact information for booking confirmations
  • Children must be secured in appropriate car seats (please request when booking)
  • Passengers are responsible for their personal belongings
  • Any damage to the vehicle caused by passengers may result in charges
