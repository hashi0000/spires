Oxford to Gatwick taxi

Pre booked runs from Oxford to Gatwick North or South terminal. Standard Car: £140 one way or £270 return when both journeys are booked together (up to 4 passengers). We confirm the fare before you travel.

Oxford to Gatwick in practice

This is a longer run than Heathrow: roughly 80 miles, usually via the M40 and M25. In light traffic you might do it in about 90 minutes. In bad M25 traffic, plan for closer to two hours. We work your pickup time back from the flight, not from a hopeful average.

Friday afternoons, Sunday evenings and holiday periods around the M25 can add a lot of time. If you have a tight check-in, tell us. We’d rather leave earlier than cut it fine.

Standard Car is £140 one way or £270 return for Oxford (up to 4 passengers). 8 Seater is £175 one way or £340 return. See airport prices from Oxford.

North or South terminal

We drop at Gatwick North or South. The fare from Oxford is the same once quoted; what matters is sending the right terminal so the driver isn’t at the wrong building.

If you’re unsure, send the airline and flight number. We’ll confirm North or South before travel.

Pickup from Oxford colleges, hotels, Headington and Summertown is included in the published Oxford fare. Kidlington, Abingdon and other nearby towns are quoted separately. Those prices are subject to change, so do not assume the Oxford rate card applies.

What’s included

  • Private hire to your Gatwick terminal
  • Fuel and standard airport approach charges
  • Fixed fare agreed in advance
  • 8 Seater £175 one way. See rates and vehicles

Arrivals back to Oxford use the same flight-monitoring approach described in how airport pickup works.

Gatwick route FAQ

North or South terminal?

Tell us your flight number. We’ll confirm the terminal. The quoted fare from Oxford is the same for either once agreed.

Is £140 one way or return?

Standard Car is £140 one way, or £270 when you book the outbound and return together. That saves £10 compared with two separate journeys.

How early should I leave for a morning flight?

It depends on check-in time and how busy the M25 is likely to be. Send your flight details and we’ll suggest a pickup time. For early flights we routinely leave Oxford well before dawn when needed.

Can I book the return from Gatwick too?

Yes. You can book a return pair online or by getting in touch.
