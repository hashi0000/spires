The journey

Oxford to Gatwick is roughly 80 miles, usually via the M40 and M25. Allow 90 minutes to 2 hours depending on time of day — we’ll suggest a pickup time based on your flight.

We drop at North or South terminal. Send your airline and flight number if you’re unsure which.

Pickup from Oxford colleges, hotels, Headington, Summertown, Kidlington, Abingdon and surrounding villages.

Gatwick route FAQ

North or South terminal?

Tell us your flight number — we’ll confirm the terminal. The fare is the same for either when quoted from Oxford.

Is £130 one-way?

£130 is our return-booking rate per leg for up to 4 passengers. One-way-only trips are quoted separately.

Can I book the return from Gatwick too?

Yes. Return pairs booked online qualify for £5 off — see offer terms.
