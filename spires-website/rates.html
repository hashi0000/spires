A small update to our airport transfer prices

Due to the current increase in fuel prices and other vehicle operating costs, we are making a small adjustment to our airport transfer fares.

We would love to continue operating at our previous prices, and we have tried to keep our fares as competitive as possible. However, with the recent rise in fuel costs, maintaining the same prices is becoming increasingly difficult without compromising the reliable service we aim to provide.

Our airport transfers will continue to be offered at a fixed price agreed at the time of booking, with no unexpected changes to your fare. We will also continue to offer a small saving when an outbound and return airport transfer are booked together.

Thank you to all of our customers, particularly those who continue to use Spires Taxis for their regular airport journeys. We greatly appreciate your continued support.

How to read airport prices on this site

Heathrow Standard Car fares are published as a one-way price and a return price when both journeys are booked together. Other airports currently show a return-booking rate per leg until their one-way and return totals are set separately.

Our airport transfer fares are fixed in advance and are regularly reviewed to reflect journey distance, airport charges and vehicle operating costs. The price confirmed at the time of booking is the price you pay, subject to any clearly stated waiting-time, parking or additional-stop charges.

Rates FAQ

What does fixed price mean?

We agree your fare before the journey starts. You are not charged by a running meter. The price confirmed at the time of booking is the price you pay, subject to any clearly stated waiting-time, parking or additional-stop charges.

Why do airport prices change from time to time?

Our airport transfer fares are fixed in advance and are regularly reviewed to reflect journey distance, airport charges and vehicle operating costs, including fuel. A confirmed quote is not changed when published prices are later reviewed.

Is airport parking included?

Yes, normal airport pickup costs are included in the quoted airport fare. Extra parking from waiting beyond the complimentary 50 minutes may be added.

How long is complimentary waiting at the airport?

Airport collections include flight tracking and up to 50 minutes of complimentary waiting from the actual landing time. After that, extra waiting is £5 per 15 minutes. Full detail: airport collection and waiting time.

How can I pay?

Card and cash. Bank transfer can be arranged in advance for some account bookings.
