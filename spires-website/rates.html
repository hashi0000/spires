Rates FAQ

What does fixed price mean?

We agree your fare before the journey starts. You are not charged by a running meter.

Is airport parking included?

Yes, normal airport pickup costs are included in the quoted airport fare.

How can I pay?

Card and cash. Bank transfer can be arranged in advance for some account bookings.
