Spires Share — Pilot Service

Direct shared airport transfer from Oxford to Heathrow Terminal 3

Spires Share is a pre booked shared private hire transfer. Passengers travelling at a similar time share a Mercedes Vito from Oxford to Heathrow Airport Terminal 3 and pay a per passenger fare. This is not a bus, coach or scheduled public transport service.

Places are offered on individual pilot departures created by Spires Taxis. Registering interest does not confirm your journey and no payment is taken at this stage.

How the pilot works

  1. Choose an available departure on this page.
  2. Sign in and register interest. Your place is provisional until the journey is confirmed.
  3. We contact you when payment opens. You pay £35 using the hosted payment page.
  4. The journey confirms when five passengers have paid, or when a four passenger departure is offered and completed.

Maximum five passengers per shared departure. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. The vehicle leaves at the stated departure time. A short grace period of up to five minutes may be possible at the driver's discretion, but this is not guaranteed. Passengers should not rely on this.

We aim to confirm the journey with five passengers. If five passengers are not secured, Spires Taxis may offer the four passenger departure option. If the required passenger numbers are not reached, the shared journey will not operate and affected passengers will be contacted regarding their payment.

You can also register interest in a shared journey when a published departure does not already exist. People travelling at a similar time are grouped together. Spires Taxis reviews that interest and may then propose an exact departure. Registering interest does not confirm a journey and no payment is taken at this stage.

Available pilot journeys

£35.00 per passenger. Oxford to Heathrow Terminal 3.

Four passenger departure option

The usual fare is £35 per passenger when five passengers travel. If only four passengers are ready, Spires Taxis may offer a four passenger departure. That option is never applied automatically.

We currently explain it as: the journey can still operate if all four passengers agree to a total fare of £43.75 each. You will already have paid £35. If everyone accepts, an additional £8.75 payment will be required.

Register or manage your place

Sign in to your Spires Taxis customer account to register interest, view your own reservation, pay when the payment window is open, accept or decline a four passenger departure, or cancel your own reservation.

Registering interest does not confirm your journey and no payment is taken at this stage.
