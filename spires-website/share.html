Spires Share

Share the journey. Split the cost.

Travelling from Oxford? Register interest in a shared journey and we'll try to match you with other passengers travelling in the same direction.

Spires Share is a pre booked shared private hire service operated by Spires Taxis. It is not a bus, coach or scheduled public transport service. We only operate a journey when there is enough compatible demand and Spires Taxis confirms it.

View interested routes Request a shared journey

How Spires Share works

  1. Register interest in a route, date and approximate time. No payment is taken.
  2. Other passengers can see aggregate interest and join if they are travelling the same way.
  3. Spires reviews the group when there is enough compatible demand and may propose an exact journey.
  4. You accept the proposal and pay only if you want that exact departure, fare and shared drop off.
  5. The journey confirms when the required passengers have paid. Payment by one passenger does not confirm the whole journey.

Interest means you would like to travel approximately on that route, date and time. A journey means Spires Taxis has proposed an exact departure, route, fare and drop off.

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. The vehicle leaves at the stated departure time. A short grace period of up to five minutes may be possible at the driver's discretion, but this is not guaranteed. Passengers should not rely on this.

Oxford to Heathrow Terminal 3

The first Spires Share pilot remains Oxford to Heathrow Terminal 3 in a Mercedes Vito. The usual model is five passengers at £35 each. If only four passengers are ready, Spires Taxis may offer a four passenger option of £43.75 each. That option is never applied automatically.

The shared drop off is Terminal 3. If your flight leaves from Terminal 2, Terminal 4 or Terminal 5, you make your own way from Terminal 3. See the Heathrow terminal transfer guide.

Register or manage your place

Sign in to your Spires Taxis customer account to request a shared journey, join interest, accept a proposed journey, pay, join a waitlist or manage your own reservation.

Registering interest does not confirm your journey and no payment is taken at this stage. This is not the same as booking a private taxi.
