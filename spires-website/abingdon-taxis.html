Taxis in Abingdon

Abingdon is one of our regular areas. Whether you're commuting to Oxford, heading to Culham Science Centre, catching a train from Radley, or need an airport run from your home in south Oxfordshire, we pre-book cars with a fixed price.

The Oxford to Abingdon run is about 15 minutes in normal traffic via the A34 or A415. We pick up from any address in Abingdon, Sutton Courtenay, Drayton and the surrounding villages.

Abingdon taxi questions

How much is Oxford to Abingdon?

Guide fare is £20 one way. This is subject to change. We confirm the exact price when you book. Return journeys are priced as two legs or a return booking. Ask when you enquire.

Do you cover Culham Science Centre?

Yes. Regular pickups for staff and visitors at Culham. Give us the building or gate when you book.

Can you pick up from Abingdon for Heathrow?

Yes. Abingdon to Heathrow is quoted from your pickup address. It is not the published Oxford airport fare, and prices from Abingdon are subject to change. Send us the full address and we will confirm the fare when you book.

How long does Oxford to Abingdon take?

About 15–20 minutes in normal traffic. Rush hour on the A34 can add time. We factor that into pickup suggestions for airport runs.
