West Oxfordshire pickups, not an Oxford start

Carterton is further west than Witney. Starting an airport transfer from here, rather than travelling east into Oxford first, keeps the journey as one pre booked car with luggage already loaded. We collect from residential streets in Carterton and from nearby villages when you send the full address.

Spires Taxis does not have a Carterton office. We are a licensed Oxford operator covering this area as a service area. Fares are quoted for the address you give us and are subject to change until confirmed.

What to send

  • Carterton pickup postcode and any access notes
  • Airport and terminal if you know it
  • Flight number for collections
  • Passengers and luggage

Carterton airport questions

How much is a taxi from Carterton to Heathrow Airport?

We do not publish a Carterton to Heathrow fare. Oxford airport prices do not apply. Send the Carterton address for a fixed quote.

Do you provide airport transfers from Carterton?

Yes. Pre booked private hire from residential addresses in Carterton and nearby west Oxfordshire villages, quoted from the door.

Can I book an 8 seater from Carterton to Heathrow?

Yes, subject to availability. Book the 8 seater for more than four passengers or heavy luggage. Typically a Mercedes Vito or similar.

Do you collect passengers from Heathrow back to Carterton?

Yes. Give us the flight number. We monitor the landing and use the same waiting rules as our other airport collections.

Do you monitor incoming flights?

Yes, on airport collections. Delays are handled from the actual landing time, not only the timetable.

Can I book a return airport transfer?

Yes. Book outbound and return together so both cars are planned, not left to a later enquiry.
