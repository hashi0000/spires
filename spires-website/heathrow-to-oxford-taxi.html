Heathrow terminals — what to tell us

We cover T2, T3, T4 and T5. Terminal layouts change, so we confirm the meet plan with your booking rather than relying on a stale map.

Terminal Useful notes for booking
Terminal 2 Many European and Star Alliance arrivals. Flight number + mobile is usually enough.
Terminal 3 Busy arrivals hall — we’ll agree how you’ll recognise the driver after reclaim.
Terminal 4 Separate from the central terminals. Say T4 if you already know it.
Terminal 5 Often British Airways. Flight number is normally enough for us to plan.

How to book your pickup

  1. Send flight details Flight number, terminal, landing date/time, passenger count, bags and full drop-off address.
  2. We confirm the fare £87 return booking on this route for up to 4 passengers, or we quote alternatives if needed.
  3. Driver on the day Your driver contacts you before pickup. Pay by card or cash unless you have an account.

Questions about this route

How long from landing to Oxford?

Allow 60–90 minutes driving from Heathrow to central Oxford in normal traffic, plus time to collect bags and meet your driver.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight. Airport collections include 50 minutes complimentary waiting from the actual landing time. Extra waiting is £5 per 15 minutes. Full detail: how airport pickup works.

Can you drop at Oxford colleges or hospitals?

Yes. Send the full address or college name when booking so we plan the right route and pickup point on the return leg if needed.

Is £87 one-way or return?

£87 is our return-booking rate per leg on Heathrow–Oxford for up to 4 passengers. One-way-only journeys are priced separately.
