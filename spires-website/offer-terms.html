Special Offer

Offer Terms & Conditions

Get £5 off your airport return journey when you sign up to our Customer Portal and book through the portal. Read the full terms and conditions below.

Terms & Conditions

About This Offer

New customers can get £5 off the total fare when they book an airport return journey with Spires Taxis. The discount is applied once per return booking (not per leg).

👤

Who Is Eligible

  • New customers signing up to the Spires Taxis Customer Portal
  • The same person must make the booking through their own account
  • Only one £5 discount per account/household may apply at our discretion
📋

How to Claim the Discount

  • Create an account in the Customer Portal via our website
  • Submit a return airport booking request (outbound + inbound) through the portal
  • Wait for us to confirm the booking by email or message
  • The £5 discount will be applied to the total confirmed fare (for example, £87 outbound + £87 return becomes £169 instead of £174)
  • Payment: You will pay the first leg fare plus half of the second leg fare upfront. The remainder will be paid on your return journey
Journey Details

What Journeys Are Included

Return journeys between the Oxford area (e.g. Oxford, Abingdon, Bicester, Kidlington etc.) and major airports, such as:

✈️

Heathrow Airport
🛫

Gatwick Airport
🛬

Luton Airport
✈️

Stansted Airport
🛫

Birmingham Airport
🛬

Bristol Airport

Important: Only applies where both the outbound and return are booked together as one return booking.

Exclusions

What Is Not Included

❌

Not Eligible

  • One-way airport trips (outbound only or return only)
  • Local trips that do not involve an airport
  • Journeys booked by phone, email, WhatsApp or any other method outside the Customer Portal
  • Bookings where another discount or promotion has already been applied
📅

Validity and End Date

  • Offer valid for bookings made up to and including 31 December 2025
  • The journey dates can be in the future, but the booking itself must be made while the offer is still active
  • We may extend or update the offer in future; any changes will be reflected on this page
Booking Terms

Changes and Cancellations

🔄

Booking Modifications

  • If the return leg is cancelled and only a single journey is taken, we reserve the right to remove the £5 discount and charge our normal fare
  • Standard waiting time, extra stops and out-of-hours charges may still apply according to our usual terms
  • If a booking is significantly changed (e.g. airport, address, times), we may need to re-quote the fare and re-apply the discount at our discretion
📜

Other Terms

  • Spires Taxis reserves the right to withdraw or amend this offer at any time for new bookings
  • The offer has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money
  • All bookings remain subject to our general terms and conditions and driver availability
