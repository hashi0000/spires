Cotswolds private tours from Oxford

A private car for your group, starting in Oxford, with stops you agree in advance. This is private hire with waiting time, not a sightseeing commentary. Guide price from £200, confirmed when you book.

Private driver, not a guided tour

We provide the vehicle and the driver. We are not qualified tour guides, we do not sell attraction tickets, and we do not run a fixed daily coach loop. You tell us which villages you want, roughly how long you want to walk around, and whether you need to be back in Oxford for a particular time.

That distinction matters. If you want historical commentary, book a specialist guide separately. If you want your own car, your own timetable, and someone waiting while you look at the high street, that is this service.

Spires Taxis is based in Oxford. We do not have offices in the Cotswolds. The day starts from your hotel, college, or home address in Oxford unless we have quoted a different pickup.

What’s included once quoted

  • Private Standard Car or 8 Seater for your group only
  • Itinerary agreed before the day
  • Waiting while you explore each stop
  • Return to Oxford, or another drop we have quoted

Cotswolds tour hire is listed from £200 on the rates page, alongside half day (£150) and full day (£280) figures. Your hours and route set the confirmed fare.

Stops people usually ask for

These are examples, not a package we insist on. Mix them, skip them, or name somewhere else when you book.

Villages we are often asked to include

  • Bourton-on-the-Water
  • Stow-on-the-Wold
  • Broadway
  • Chipping Campden
  • Bibury

How the day is built

Send three or four places and a finish time. We will say if that is realistic in the hours you have. Extra stops on a quoted day hire need to be agreed so the timing still works. We do not hold restaurant tables or attraction entry.

Cars, hours and coming back

Standard Car is comfortable for up to four. Families or friends who want to sit together without splitting across two vehicles should book the 8 Seater.

If you only need a one-way drop to a single village, say so. That is quoted as a point-to-point journey, not the tour rate. Visitors staying in Oxford often want the round trip so they are not hunting for a return taxi in a village at 5pm.

Day hire figures (half day, full day, hourly from £35) are on rates. Student discount is available across our services if you mention it and bring ID. See also long distance taxis and about us.

What to send when you book

  • Date and pickup address in Oxford
  • Number of passengers
  • Places you want to visit, even if approximate
  • How long you have, and where you want to finish

Cotswolds hire FAQ

Is this a guided tour?

No. We supply the private car and driver. We are not tour guides and we do not provide a commentary as part of the hire.

How much is a Cotswolds tour from Oxford?

From £200 as a guide on our rates page. We confirm the fare for your date, hours and route when you book.

Can we choose different villages?

Yes. Bourton-on-the-Water, Stow-on-the-Wold, Broadway, Chipping Campden and Bibury are common requests, not a fixed itinerary.

Do you wait while we walk around?

Yes. Tell us roughly how long you want at each stop so the day still finishes when you need it to.

Plan a Cotswolds day from Oxford

Send the date, passenger count and the places you have in mind. We confirm the fare before you travel.
