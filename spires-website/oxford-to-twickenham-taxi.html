What the return fare covers

  • One outward journey and one return
  • The same Oxford address at both ends
  • Agreed collection and return times
  • Standard Car or 8 Seater, priced for the vehicle

Other Oxfordshire pickup locations and additional stops need a tailored quote. Ask us if you are not sure your address is the Oxford fare. We have not published a postcode boundary, a parking inclusion or a waiting allowance. Those are agreed with the itinerary before you travel.

Arrival and the journey home

Tell us when you want to arrive and when you want to be collected afterwards. We then agree an Oxford collection time that aims for that arrival. Journey time depends on your address, the date and the traffic. We do not guarantee it.

Road closures may mean a walk from an agreed accessible meeting point. We do not promise to collect you directly outside the stadium.

The vehicle is not reserved exclusively between the drop off and the return unless that is arranged separately. On a finals day with two matches, say if you are going to one or both. We can collect you before the first and bring you home after the second. The car does not stay at the ground in between unless we quote that.

Questions

How much is a taxi from Oxford to Twickenham?

A Standard Car return from an Oxford address to Allianz Stadium is £230. An 8 Seater return is £295. The price is for the whole vehicle on a listed November 2026 match date, subject to availability and an agreed itinerary.

How much is a return taxi from Oxford to Twickenham?

The published fares are return fares: £230 for a Standard Car and £295 for an 8 Seater. Each fare covers one outward journey and one return from the same Oxford address.

Can I book an 8 seater for Twickenham rugby?

Yes. An 8 Seater takes up to 8 passengers and is £295 return. Shared by eight people that is about £36.88 each. You book the vehicle, not a single seat.

Does Spires Taxis provide return transfers?

Yes. The return collection is agreed before you travel. The vehicle is not held at the stadium between the journeys unless that is arranged separately.

Can I book for England rugby internationals?

Yes. This page lists the November 2026 Nations Championship matches at Allianz Stadium, including England v Australia, England v Japan, England v New Zealand and the finals weekend. A request is confirmed only after a quote is accepted.

Can I arrange pickup after the match?

Yes. Tell us the return time you want. The meeting point near the stadium is agreed with you. Road closures may mean a short walk. We do not promise collection directly outside the stadium.

Does Spires Taxis serve areas outside central Oxford?

The published fare is for an address in Oxford. Pickups elsewhere in Oxfordshire, and any extra stop, need a separate quote.

How far in advance should I book?

Match days bring large crowds. Arrange the return before you travel so the collection is agreed. A vehicle is not held until the quote is accepted.
