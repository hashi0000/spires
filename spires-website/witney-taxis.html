Witney Taxi & Airport Transfers

Spires Taxis is an Oxford private hire operator. We provide pre booked journeys serving Witney. The fare is agreed before you travel. We do not keep a taxi rank or a permanently stationed driver in Witney.

Taxi transfers between Witney and Oxford

Witney is the main town on the A40 west of Oxford. We collect from the High Street and Woolgate area, Windrush Place, and home addresses across the town. Oxford bookings are often for the hospitals, the universities, Oxford station and evening returns.

The nearest station is Hanborough, which has its own range when that is the actual pickup. A Witney town centre fare is not automatically the fare from an outlying village.

Witney to central Oxford is often about 30 to 45 minutes via the A40.

Questions about Witney

How much is a taxi from Witney to Oxford?

For a typical pre booked Standard Car journey between Oxford and Witney, the indicative one way range is £35–£55. That is not a guaranteed fare or a price cap. The confirmed price can be above or below the range, and a journey in the opposite direction is quoted on its own if the operating cost differs.

Can I book an airport transfer from Witney?

Yes. We can quote a pre booked airport transfer from Witney to Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton, Birmingham, Stansted and other airports. Fixed fares for Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted and Luton are on the Witney airport transfers page. Oxford airport prices do not apply. Other airports are quoted from your pickup address and are subject to change.

Do you offer an 8 Seater?

Yes. A Mercedes Vito is available for suitable group bookings, subject to availability. Regional 8 Seater journeys are quoted individually. The Standard Car range on this page does not apply to the 8 Seater.

Can I get a fixed quote before booking?

Yes. We agree the fare before you travel. Send the pickup, destination, date and time and we will confirm the price before you book.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Cash and card are both accepted. If you have not decided yet, say so when you enquire and we will confirm the payment method with the fare.

How far in advance should I book?

Earlier is better for early morning pickups, airport journeys and busy dates. A booking is only confirmed once we have agreed the fare and accepted the job. We do not keep cars stationed in every town.
