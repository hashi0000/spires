Event transfers from Oxford

Pre booked cars for rugby, motorsport and other busy days. Where a fare is published, it is on that page. Every other venue is quoted for the date, the address and the vehicle.

Featured

Twickenham Rugby | Allianz Stadium

Spires Taxis provides pre booked transport from Oxford for England rugby matches and other events at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham. Book the whole vehicle. The return collection is agreed before you travel.

£230 return

Standard Car, up to 4 passengers. About £57.50 each when four share the car.

£295 return

8 Seater, up to 8 passengers. About £36.88 each when eight share the vehicle.

Advance booking is recommended on match days. A request is confirmed only after a quote is accepted.

Where a page already exists

Oxford to Allianz Stadium

Twickenham rugby transfers for England matches. Standard Car £230 return. 8 Seater £295 return.

Silverstone and motorsport

Race weekends and other meetings at the circuit. The fare is quoted for your date. There is no published circuit price on this page.

Other busy days

Concerts, stadiums and festivals do not have their own pages yet. Send the venue and we will quote the journey.

Concerts and stadiums

Tell us the venue, the date and how many people are travelling. We agree the drop off and the return collection before you go. We do not promise a stop at a particular gate.

Festivals and major events

Large events change the roads on the day. We quote from your Oxford address and do not publish a journey time for those days.

Group travel

A Standard Car takes up to 4 passengers. An 8 Seater takes up to 8. You book the vehicle, not a single seat. See our vehicles.
