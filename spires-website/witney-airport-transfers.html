Witney airport taxi, collected from your door

Spires Taxis provides pre booked private hire from home addresses, workplaces and guest stays in Witney. The car comes to you. You do not need a lift into Oxford station or a coach stop to start the airport journey.

Witney sits on the A40 west of Oxford. For a taxi from Witney to Heathrow that usually means heading towards the M40 without a detour through the city. Published airport fares on this website are for Oxford only. A Witney pickup is quoted from the full address and confirmed when you book.

Taxi from Witney to Heathrow

Most Witney airport taxi bookings are Heathrow. Send the terminal if you know it, or the flight number if you do not. We drop at the correct building and, for collections, we track the flight rather than waiting on the original schedule.

The Oxford to Heathrow page explains terminals and the published Oxford fare. That Oxford figure does not apply from Witney. For the other direction, the same rule holds: a Heathrow collection back to Witney is quoted as its own journey.

No Witney Heathrow price is published. Oxford Standard Car fares on the rates page are Oxford journeys only. Send the Witney address for a quote.

Airport pickup from Heathrow to Witney

Give us the flight number when you book. We monitor the actual landing time. You then have 50 minutes from landing to clear immigration, collect luggage and tell us you are ready. The driver makes their way to the pickup point after that message. Extra waiting is £10 per additional 30 minutes or part thereof.

Full wording: how airport pickup works. The fare we confirm is the fare you pay unless you change the route or add stops.

Why book a private transfer from Witney

Getting into Oxford first, then starting the airport run, adds a change with cases. A pre booked car from Ducklington, Cogges, the town centre or the A40 employment areas keeps the journey as one booking, timed from your street, including early departures.

Witney airport transfer questions

How much is a taxi from Witney to Heathrow Airport?

There is no published Witney to Heathrow fare on our rate card. Oxford airport prices do not apply. Send your Witney pickup address, flight details and passenger count and we will confirm a fixed quote.

Do you provide airport transfers from Witney?

Yes. Spires Taxis provides pre booked private airport transfers from Witney and nearby west Oxfordshire addresses. You do not need to travel into Oxford first.

Can I book an 8 seater from Witney to Heathrow?

Yes, subject to availability. The 8 seater is typically a Mercedes Vito or similar. Tell us passenger and luggage numbers when you enquire so we quote the right vehicle class.

Do you collect passengers from Heathrow back to Witney?

Yes. Book the Heathrow collection with your flight number. We monitor the landing time and use the same waiting policy as our other airport pickups.

Do you monitor incoming flights?

Yes, on airport collections. Give us the flight number when you book. We track the actual landing time and adjust the driver if the flight is delayed or early.

Can I book a return airport transfer?

Yes. Book outbound and return together so both legs are confirmed. The return from the airport still needs the flight number for monitoring.
