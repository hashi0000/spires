Taxi transfers between Wantage and Oxford

Wantage market place and the residential streets around Charlton and the new estates are the usual pickups. Oxford journeys are often for the hospitals, the universities and Oxford station. Grove is next door and has its own range.

There is no mainline station in the town. If you want Didcot Parkway or Oxford station, say which one. We quote the full address rather than a flat town fare.

Wantage to Oxford is often about 35 to 50 minutes.

Questions about Wantage

How much is a taxi from Wantage to Oxford?

For a typical pre booked Standard Car journey between Oxford and Wantage, the indicative one way range is £40–£60. That is not a guaranteed fare or a price cap. The confirmed price can be above or below the range, and a journey in the opposite direction is quoted on its own if the operating cost differs.

Can I book an airport transfer from Wantage?

Yes. We can quote a pre booked airport transfer from Wantage to Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton, Birmingham, Stansted and other airports. There is no published airport fare for this town. Oxford airport prices do not apply. Ask us for a quote from your pickup address.

Do you offer an 8 Seater?

Yes. A Mercedes Vito is available for suitable group bookings, subject to availability. Regional 8 Seater journeys are quoted individually. The Standard Car range on this page does not apply to the 8 Seater.

Can I get a fixed quote before booking?

Yes. We agree the fare before you travel. Send the pickup, destination, date and time and we will confirm the price before you book.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Cash and card are both accepted. If you have not decided yet, say so when you enquire and we will confirm the payment method with the fare.

How far in advance should I book?

Earlier is better for early morning pickups, airport journeys and busy dates. A booking is only confirmed once we have agreed the fare and accepted the job. We do not keep cars stationed in every town.
