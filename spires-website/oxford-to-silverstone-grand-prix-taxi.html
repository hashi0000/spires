Silverstone: Where Formula 1 History Began

From a former RAF airfield to one of the world's most celebrated racing circuits, Silverstone has played a central role in British motorsport for generations.

1950First Formula 1 World Championship race

From RAF Airfield to Racing Circuit

Silverstone was developed on the site of a Second World War RAF airfield. The circuit opened with a Grand Prix in 1948, before the Formula 1 World Championship existed.

Ferrari's First Formula 1 Victory

José Froilán González secured Ferrari's first Formula 1 World Championship victory at Silverstone on 14 July 1951, finishing about 51 seconds ahead of Juan Manuel Fangio.

Lewis Hamilton's 2008 Victory

Lewis Hamilton won the 2008 British Grand Prix in wet conditions, by 68.577 seconds from Nick Heidfeld. That race used an earlier Silverstone layout, with 60 laps rather than the current 52.

The official race distance on the current layout is 306.198 km. That figure comes from 52 laps of the 5.891 km circuit, with the published start and finish offset.

Getting to Silverstone from Oxford

Grand Prix traffic can be significantly heavier than a normal Oxford journey. Temporary road restrictions may operate, and some roads may run one way. Access to the circuit can be restricted, and an official shuttle may be required for the last part of the trip. Evening collections can take longer than the journey out.

Collections may be arranged from an authorised meeting point, potentially Towcester Racecourse, subject to the official 2027 arrangements. We do not guarantee direct access to the circuit gates, and we do not promise a journey time during Grand Prix traffic. The collection point is confirmed before you travel.

You will have a driver contact number with a confirmed booking. If the race runs late, or you want to stay for the concert, call or message that driver. A change of time is agreed with us. It is not automatic. Shuttle tickets, where they are required, are separate from the private hire fare.

Grand Prix questions

How much is a taxi from Oxford to Silverstone for the British Grand Prix?

From central Oxford, Friday 2 July to Sunday 4 July 2027, Essential starts at £975 for a Standard Car and £1,275 for an 8 seater. Dedicated starts at £1,785 and £2,250. Other pickup points are quoted separately. Sending the form does not confirm the fare.

Can I book a taxi for all three Grand Prix days?

Yes. The published packages cover 2 to 4 July 2027. We confirm the times and whether a driver is available before travel.

Can I book transport for just one day?

Yes, as a separate quote. The published totals are for the three days together, not a one day fare.

Can I arrange collection after the evening concerts?

You can ask for a later evening collection. We agree the time with the quote. We do not guarantee a particular pickup time or circuit access until that is confirmed.

Do you provide an 8 seater for Silverstone?

Yes, subject to availability. It takes up to eight passengers and is typically a Mercedes Vito or a similar vehicle.

Can eight passengers share the fare?

The price is for the vehicle. If all eight seats are used on Essential, £1,275 is about £159.38 per passenger for the weekend, or about £53.13 per passenger per day. Empty seats do not reduce the fare.

Will my driver wait at Silverstone?

On Essential, no. The driver returns for the agreed evening collection. On Dedicated, the driver is reserved for up to 10 hours a day, including the journey from Oxford. Waiting depends on authorised parking and the event rules. It is not unlimited.

What is the difference between Essential and Dedicated?

Essential is a morning transfer and an evening return at agreed times. Dedicated reserves the vehicle and driver for up to 10 hours a day, with more flexibility during that period.

Where will my driver collect me?

From the address you give us in Oxford or Oxfordshire. At Silverstone, the meeting point is confirmed before travel and may be an authorised point such as Towcester Racecourse.

Can you take passengers directly to the circuit?

We do not guarantee the circuit gates. Roads can be restricted, and some arrivals use an official shuttle.

Are shuttle tickets included?

No. Where a shuttle ticket is required, it is separate from the private hire fare unless we specifically confirm otherwise.

How early should we leave Oxford?

Race weekend traffic is heavier than a normal day. We do not publish a journey time. We agree a departure time when we quote.

What happens if the race finishes late?

Contact the driver on the number given with the booking. A later collection can be discussed. It is not automatic, and a Dedicated driver is available only within the agreed duty period.

Can I book from Bicester or Banbury?

Yes. The published prices are for central Oxford. Bicester, Banbury and other towns are quoted from the pickup address.

Is a deposit required?

The form does not take a deposit. If a booking is agreed, we will say what is due before travel. Our terms also allow payment in the vehicle.

Can I book multiple vehicles?

You can ask. Extra vehicles depend on driver availability and are not confirmed until we accept the booking.

Are the prices per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Sharing the cost is up to your group.

Can I request a different return time each day?

Yes. Add Friday, Saturday and Sunday times on the form. Essential uses the agreed times. Dedicated stays within the reserved duty period.
