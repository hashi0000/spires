Oxford to London City Airport taxi

Pre-booked transfers from Oxford to London City Airport (LCY). Standard Car: £150 one way or £290 return when both journeys are booked together (up to 4 passengers). Airport charges are included.

Why London City is a different job from Heathrow

London City sits in the Royal Docks, not on the M25/M4 corridor we use for Heathrow. It is a single-terminal airport used heavily for short-haul European flights. From Oxford, a lot of the bookings we take are business trips: early outbound, evening return, one cabin bag, and a need for the fare to be agreed before anyone leaves the office.

It is still a pre-booked private hire journey. We collect from your Oxford address, drop at the terminal, and for the way home we monitor the flight in the same way as our other airport collections. See how airport pickup works.

Standard Car is £150 one way or £290 return for Oxford (up to 4 passengers). 8 Seater is £190 one way or £370 return. Full rate card.

What’s included

  • Pre-booked private hire to London City Airport
  • Fixed fare confirmed before travel
  • Airport charges included
  • Flight monitoring on return pickups
  • 8 Seater £190 one way. See rates · vehicles

One terminal, still send the flight number

There is no North/South choice as at Gatwick. Send the flight number anyway so we can time an arrival collection and so the driver is not guessing from a scheduled time that has already slipped.

Business travel and luggage

London City suits people who want a smaller airport and a Docklands drop, not a west-London terminal. Luggage is still the thing that catches people out.

Typical bookings from Oxford

Science park and city-centre offices, colleges hosting visiting academics, and hotel pickups for guests flying out of LCY rather than Heathrow. Account invoicing can be set up for regular travellers: corporate taxis.

Bags

One or two people with cabin bags fit the Standard Car easily. Four passengers with hold luggage should say so. If cases will not sit comfortably with four adults, book the 8 Seater. You book the class, not a guaranteed model.

Returns to Oxford

Book the collection with the flight number. We track the actual landing time. You have 50 minutes from landing to clear the airport and tell us you are ready; the driver then makes their way to the pickup point. Extra waiting after that is £10 per additional 30 minutes or part thereof. Full wording: airport collection waiting policy.

Pickup from Oxford colleges, hotels, Headington and Summertown is included in the published Oxford fare. Kidlington, Abingdon and other nearby towns are quoted separately and those prices are subject to change.

Heathrow, Gatwick and Luton remain our busiest airport pages. London City is the Docklands option on the same airport transfers list.

What to send when you book

  • Pickup address in Oxford
  • Flight number and date
  • Passengers and bags
  • Whether you want the return booked together

Nearby towns: do not assume the Oxford figures apply. Send the full address. Contact or the customer portal.

London City Airport FAQ

How much is Oxford to London City Airport?

Standard Car is £150 one way or £290 return for up to 4 passengers. 8 Seater is £190 one way or £370 return. Oxford journeys only; nearby towns are quoted separately.

Is there more than one terminal?

No. One terminal in the Royal Docks. Still send the flight number for collections.

Do you monitor the inbound flight?

Yes. Same approach as our other airport work: we track the landing time and include 50 minutes from actual landing. See how airport pickup works.

Can I book this for a business account?

Yes. Regular LCY runs can go on account invoicing. Corporate taxis explains how that is set up.
