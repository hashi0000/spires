Pre booked private hire from Wallingford and the Thames corridor. A taxi from Wallingford to Heathrow does not need a detour into Oxford first. Gatwick and Luton are quoted the same way, from your address.

Why Wallingford is a different start point from Oxford

Wallingford sits south of Oxford on the Thames, with the A4074 towards Oxford and the A4130 towards Didcot and the wider south Oxfordshire road network. For Heathrow, going north into the city first is usually the wrong direction. We collect from the town and nearby villages and run the airport journey as one booking.

Spires Taxis is a licensed Oxford private hire operator. We do not have an office in Wallingford. This page is a service area: pre booked cars from your Wallingford address, with a fare agreed before travel.

Included on airport collections

  • Flight monitoring from the number you give us
  • 50 minutes from actual landing to tell us you are ready
  • Clear pickup plan before the driver sets off into the airport
  • Fixed price confirmed when you book

Wallingford to Heathrow taxi

Tell us the terminal or the flight number. We drop at Terminals 2, 3, 4 or 5. Oxford published Heathrow fares on the Oxford to Heathrow page do not apply to Wallingford pickups. The same is true for a collection at Heathrow back to Wallingford: it is quoted from your drop address, not taken from the Oxford rate card.

If you are comparing a drive into Oxford to pick up a published Oxford fare, ask us to quote the Wallingford address instead. For most people with luggage, one private hire booking from home is the simpler plan.

Wallingford airport questions

How much is a taxi from Wallingford to Heathrow Airport?

We do not publish a Wallingford to Heathrow fare. Oxford airport prices do not apply. Send the Wallingford pickup address and flight details for a fixed quote.

Do you provide airport transfers from Wallingford?

Yes. We collect from Wallingford town and nearby addresses in Cholsey, Benson and along the Thames corridor. Journeys are pre booked private hire.

Can I book an 8 seater from Wallingford to Heathrow?

Yes, subject to availability. Ask for the 8 seater if you have more than four passengers or a lot of luggage. It is typically a Mercedes Vito or similar.

Do you collect passengers from Heathrow back to Wallingford?

Yes. Book the collection with your flight number. We monitor landing time and follow our standard airport waiting policy.

Do you monitor incoming flights?

Yes, for airport collections. The driver is timed to the actual landing, not only the original schedule.

Can I book a return airport transfer?

Yes. Confirm outbound and return together. Each airport collection still needs the flight number.
