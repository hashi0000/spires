When students book us

Michaelmas and Hilary term starts are our busiest weeks. Parents dropping off at college, students arriving from Heathrow with two suitcases and a guitar case, groups sharing a car to Gatwick at the end of term — we’ve done all of it.

We pick up from University of Oxford colleges and halls, Oxford Brookes sites around Headington, shared houses in Cowley and Jericho, Oxford station and the airports. Tell us your college name or full address when you book — “near the High Street” isn’t enough for a reliable meet.

Student discount is available across our services. Mention it when booking and have your student ID ready on the day. If you’re not sure whether you need the standard car or the Vito for luggage, see our vehicles or ask on WhatsApp.

Typical student journeys

  • Heathrow or Gatwick to college — from £95 / £130
  • College to Oxford station or bus station
  • Parents' weekend — station to college and back
  • End-of-term airport runs with luggage

How to book

  1. Message us Date, pickup (college or address), destination, passengers and bags. Mention student discount.
  2. We confirm the fare Fixed price including any student discount — Heathrow from £95 one way, Gatwick from £130.
  3. On the day Driver contacts you before pickup. Bring valid student ID. Pay by card or cash.

Student taxi questions

How does the student discount work?

Mention it when you book — by WhatsApp, phone or online. Bring valid student ID on the day. The discount applies across our services; we'll confirm the adjusted fare before you travel.

Can four students share one car to Heathrow?

Yes. Our Standard Car fare from Oxford to Heathrow is £95 one way or £185 return for up to 4 passengers — that's often cheaper per person than the train with luggage.

Do you know where Oxford colleges are?

Yes. We regularly pick up from colleges, halls and student houses across Oxford. Give us the college name or full address.

Can parents book a car for drop-off day?

Absolutely. Parents often book the Heathrow or station pickup and the college drop-off as separate legs or a return. Contact us with your dates.
