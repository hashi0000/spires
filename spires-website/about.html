About us

A taxi company built around Oxford airport work

Spires Taxis started because Oxford needed a dependable pre-booked service with clear pricing. Most of our jobs are airport runs, but we also handle local trips and longer journeys across Oxfordshire and beyond.

Our story

Set up to solve a simple problem

Too many people were getting mixed prices or uncertain availability when travelling to the airport. We built Spires Taxis to offer something straightforward: pre-booked cars, clear fares, and proper communication before the journey starts.

Our drivers know Oxford well, from college areas and hospitals to the ring road and the M40 corridor to Heathrow. That local knowledge helps us plan sensible pickup times and routes, especially during busy periods.

  1. Book ahead Share your date, time, pickup and destination.
  2. Receive a fixed fare We confirm the price before the journey.
  3. Travel with updates We stay in touch and track flights for airport arrivals.
