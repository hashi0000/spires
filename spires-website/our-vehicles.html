Vehicles for Oxford airport runs

We match the car to the job: number of people, luggage, and whether you’re going to Heathrow at 4am or collecting a family from Gatwick.

See prices by vehicle Ask which car you need

Standard Car

Toyota RAV4 / Corolla or similar

Suitable for up to four passengers with normal holiday luggage. This is the class most airport bookings use.

You book the vehicle class, not a guaranteed make and model.

Priced as Standard Car on our Oxford airport rate card. Oxford to Heathrow: £92 one way / £180 return.

8 Seater

Mercedes Vito or similar

We normally recommend the 8 Seater for larger groups with several suitcases. It also helps when four adults each have large cases and you don’t want to force everything into a smaller boot. Cruise sailings from Southampton and group runs to Silverstone are typical examples.

Airport 8 Seater prices are listed on the airport transfer prices page. Oxford to Heathrow, for example, is £120 one way or £235 return. Route detail: Oxford to Heathrow taxi.

Tell us passenger count and bag count when you enquire.

Vehicle models are examples only and may vary depending on availability and booking requirements. Luggage capacity depends on passenger numbers. Please tell us about large or additional luggage when booking.

How to choose

If you’re unsure, message us on WhatsApp with:

  • Number of adults and children
  • Number of large cases and cabin bags
  • Any sports kit, pushchairs or odd-shaped items

We’ll say whether the Standard Car is fine or whether the 8 Seater is the sensible option. Getting this right before travel is easier than rearranging on the day.

For airport runs see airport transfers and the Oxford to Heathrow page. Larger groups sharing a car are usually priced as one vehicle on the rates page. Corporate account bookings are on corporate taxis.

Child seats

Child seats can be arranged subject to availability. There’s a small charge on the rates page. Tell us the child’s age when you book so we bring the right seat.

Licensing

Spires Taxis is a licensed private hire operator with Oxford City Council. Journeys are pre-booked. Drivers and vehicles used on licensed work meet the requirements that apply to private hire in Oxford.

Vehicle questions

When should I book the 8 Seater?

If you have more than four passengers, or four passengers with a lot of luggage, for example several large suitcases plus cabin bags. The 8 Seater is typically a Mercedes Vito or similar.

Can I request a specific car?

We allocate a suitable licensed vehicle for the booking. If you need the 8 Seater, say so when you book so we price and plan for that class.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, subject to availability, for the charge listed on our rates page. Tell us ages when you book.

Not sure which vehicle?

Send passenger and luggage details. We’ll confirm the fare and the right vehicle class before you travel.
