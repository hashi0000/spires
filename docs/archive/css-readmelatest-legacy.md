## 🏢 Admin System Features

### **Fleet Management Dashboard**
- **Real-time Statistics**: Total bookings, today's bookings, active bookings, active drivers
- **Professional Navigation**: Easy switching between Bookings, Drivers, Vehicles, Reports
- **System Status**: 3-year license tracking and compliance monitoring

### **Booking Management** 📅
- ✅ **Add New Bookings**: Complete form with customer details, journey information
- ✅ **View Bookings**: Detailed booking information display
- ✅ **Edit Bookings**: Real-time status updates (completed, active, cancelled)
- ✅ **Search & Filter**: By customer, date, status, destination
- ✅ **Export Data**: CSV export for business records

### **Driver Management** 👤
- ✅ **Driver Registration**: Complete forms with all required documentation
- ✅ **License Tracking**: Automatic expiry monitoring and alerts
- ✅ **DBS Management**: Check dates with automatic 3-year renewal tracking
- ✅ **Medical Certificates**: Expiry tracking and renewal alerts
- ✅ **Status Management**: Active, inactive, suspended, pending approval
- ✅ **Performance Tracking**: Trip counts and completion rates
- ✅ **Document Alerts**: 30/90 day expiry warnings with color coding

### **Vehicle Fleet Management** 🚗
- ✅ **Vehicle Registration**: Complete vehicle profiles with documentation
- ✅ **MOT Tracking**: Automatic expiry monitoring with alerts
- ✅ **Insurance Management**: Policy tracking and renewal alerts
- ✅ **Service Scheduling**: Automatic 6-month service intervals
- ✅ **Driver Assignment**: Link vehicles to active drivers
- ✅ **Mileage Tracking**: Current mileage and utilization monitoring
- ✅ **Status Management**: Active, maintenance, inactive
