## 2) GOOGLE REVIEWS

### Get Your Review Link
1. Go to your Google Business Profile
2. Click on "Get more reviews" or "Share review form"
3. Copy the direct review link (it will look like: `https://g.page/r/.../review`)

### Request Reviews from Customers
Send this message to satisfied customers (via WhatsApp, email, or text):

**Template Message:**
```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for choosing Spires Taxis! We hope you had a great experience with us.

If you have a moment, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review. It helps other customers find us and helps us improve our service.

[Paste your review link here]

Thank you so much!

Best regards,
Spires Taxis
```

### When to Ask for Reviews
- Immediately after a successful journey (while the experience is fresh)
- After airport transfers (customers often have time to review)
- After positive feedback via phone/WhatsApp
- Don't ask too frequently - once per customer is enough

### Respond to Reviews
- Always respond to reviews (both positive and negative)
- Thank customers for positive reviews
- Address any concerns in negative reviews professionally
- This shows you're engaged and care about customer service

## 3) GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE

### Add Your Website
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add Property"
4. Enter your website URL: `https://www.spires-taxis.com`
5. Choose "URL prefix" as the property type

### Verify Ownership
You have two options:

**Option A: HTML Tag (Easiest)**
1. Google will provide an HTML tag (looks like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="..."/>`)
2. Add this tag to the `<head>` section of your `index.html` file
3. Click "Verify" in Search Console

**Option B: DNS Verification**
1. If you have access to your domain's DNS settings
2. Add a TXT record as instructed by Google
3. Click "Verify" in Search Console

### Submit Your Sitemap
1. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"
4. Google will start crawling your site (this can take a few days)

### Monitor Performance
- Check "Performance" regularly to see:
  - Which keywords people search for to find you
  - Which pages get the most clicks
  - Your average position in search results
- Use this data to improve your content over time
